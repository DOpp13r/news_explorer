# News Explorer

# Overview
This project is a combination of many tools utilized throughout the course! It starts with the idea of simple page design layout while bringing in the factor of styling for a pleasing experience. The interactive project pertains news articles with numerous topics from all over the world! If the article(s) seem interesting, why not create your own account and keep that article always ready, whether it is to view some time later on, or on the go through your phone. The article is just a few simple clicks away and always ready in your account page. The endless arsenal of articles are yours to explorer!
